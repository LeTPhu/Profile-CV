QA-TIGER Robustness.

Robustness in multimodal VQA

A study of robustness under missing modalities using modality dropout, knowledge distillation and quality gates.

Academic research

01 / Project overview

Scope & contributions

  • Studied robustness with missing modalities using modality dropout, knowledge distillation, quality gates and missing tokens.
  • In the recorded evaluation setup, Missing Visual increased from 51.57% to 65.38%.

Results recorded in the CV

The CV records an increase of +13.80 percentage points in that evaluation setup; these figures are not presented as a general result across benchmarks.

Technologies

  • Multimodal VQA
  • Modality Dropout
  • Knowledge Distillation
  • Quality Gate
  • Missing Tokens
